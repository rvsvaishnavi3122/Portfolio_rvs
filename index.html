03 · Career journey

My path into product growth.

I started in QA and gradually took on more product work. Here is what prompted that shift.

Where it began

QA helped me see the product through the customer’s experience.

While testing onboarding and policy journeys at Deloitte, I started looking beyond whether a flow worked. Funnel analysis and user research helped me understand where customers dropped off, what was getting in their way, and which changes could help them move forward.

Why I chose product: I wanted to work earlier in the process: understand customer needs, decide what to improve, and help teams deliver the change. I began taking on product analytics, story prioritization, requirements, and cross-functional delivery alongside my QA work.

The shift brought together the parts of the work I found most meaningful: solving customer problems, using evidence to guide decisions, and seeing improvements reach production.

Nov 2023 to Present

Software Engineer in Test · Product Growth & Experimentation

Deloitte
